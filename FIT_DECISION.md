# Current accepted fit intent

The four symmetric nubs stay centered on their confirmed corner spans and extend the existing hex ridges. The intended flex remains inward toward the pocket, perpendicular to the clip broad face. Full walls remain coupled to the backing; no isolation groove or free tongue is added.

The revised hidden seat provides crest clearance and minor engagement on the two diagonal flanks. Its nominal local rigid-envelope inward clearance target is 0.02257 mm, replacing the previous 0.02730 mm target. This number is not measured installed deformation or guaranteed printed preload.

The clip and both shells slice using Bambu PETG Basic with the Bambu X2D 0.4 mm profile, 0.12 mm main layers. Physical seating, bending direction, insertion passage, residual preload, retention and endurance still need paired print testing. The current linear strain screening does not qualify the insertion mechanism or material strength.
