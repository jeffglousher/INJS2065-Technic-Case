# Face setback, compact adapter, and standard pin clearance

Latest request: move the first side row back one 8 mm Technic increment, retain tight nominal fits and a short rotating adapter; housing may be taller if necessary.

The verified v16.13 standard-pin layout uses side rows Z30/Z46, bearing Z35.30 to Z38.80, and housing front Z50 (4 mm setback). Keeping those pin clearances while changing setback to 12 mm puts the housing front at Z58. Moving the rows down instead returns the lower pin to the servo's occupied volume and 5.5 mm insertion limit.

A short adapter can end at Z42 while keeping its full 8 mm cross socket (bottom Z34), reinforced horn end, and existing bearing location. This gives an adapter height of 15.7 mm and a 16 mm recess behind the Z58 front face. An axle would traverse that recess before engaging the socket. If the output must remain flush, the adapter must extend to the Z58 front face instead.

The front-face bores should be 8 mm deep, leaving 1.475 mm between their ends and the upper side-bores. Side pitch remains 16 x 16 mm; front pattern remains (+/-8, +/-8); both patterns retain the same 8 mm grid phase around the corner.

These are nominal CAD dimensions and geometric checks, not measured printer capability or a certified GD&T result. Print fit depends on PETG process variation.
