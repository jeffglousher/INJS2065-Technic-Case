# Sources

- [Bambu PETG Basic technical data sheet](https://store.bblcdn.com/s1/default/cb94589bf7994fdcbfa833badefae9cd/Bambu_PETG_Basic_Technical_Data_Sheet.pdf): material screening information. Coupon properties do not qualify this holder's retention or lifetime.
- [Covestro snap-fit design guide](https://solutions.covestro.com/-/media/covestro/solution-center/brands/downloads/imported/1556891135.pdf?hash=0B0654C1EA0B882DEB401F521C4E87DE&rev=75179dfd38264e398477a19a84601af5): snap-fit concepts. A cantilever formula is not a direct model of this backed clip.
- [Yoshida and Wada, Mechanics of a snap fit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13566v2): elasticity, friction and geometry in engagement and disengagement.
- [Bambu Studio](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio): native 3MF settings and slicing. The project contains original configuration and checking scripts, not copied slicer implementation.
- [Limo CAD](https://github.com/limo-cad/Limo-CAD): editable CAD application.
- [OCCT documentation](https://dev.opencascade.org/doc/overview/html/): exact-solid sections, distances and interference queries.

External publications and figures remain subject to their own licenses. Printed
fit, load retention, creep and fatigue need physical qualification.
